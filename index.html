James Cortis

ames (born Zhao Yufan on October 14, 2005) is a dancer, songwriter, and producer under BigHit Music (HYBE). He is best known as a member of the K-pop boy group CORTIS.

Career Highlights

Highlights of James

Prdebut

Pre-Debut (Trainee A): Before debuting with CORTIS, James was widely known as a member of Trainee A, BigHit Music's pre-debut boy group project before its disbandment in late 2022. He remained with BigHit Music following the project.

Dancer & Performer

Known for his dance background, he previously performed as a backup dancer for BTS's Jungkook during his solo promotions for "Seven"

Songwriting & Production

Prior to and during his group activities, James gained song credits as a songwriter/producer on notable HYBE releases, including TXT's "Deja Vu" and ILLIT's "Magnetic".
